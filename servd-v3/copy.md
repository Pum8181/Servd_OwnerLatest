## How it works (4 steps)

01. **Guest scans and orders** — They scan the table's QR, browse the
    live menu, pick spice levels, and tag their name — no app to install.
02. **It pauses for a human check** — The order lands in a Needs
    Confirmation queue, kept clearly separate from anything already in
    the kitchen.
03. **One tap, it's confirmed** — Staff catch anything wrong, fix it if
    needed, and confirm — now it's genuinely in the kitchen's queue.
04. **Bill however they want** — One bill, split evenly, or split per
    person — chosen in one tap, clear enough to read aloud at the table.

## Features (section: "The Full Menu")

- **Spice & Variant Selector** — Mild, Medium, Hot, Extra Hot per dish,
  with optional price bumps — upsells happen automatically.
- **Human Confirmation Step** — Nothing reaches the kitchen
  automatically. A person reviews and taps confirm first.
- **Per-Guest Order Tracking** — Know exactly who ordered what, even
  when the whole table scanned the same QR code.
- **Flexible Split Billing** — Together, split evenly, or split per
  person — chosen in one tap, no table-side math.
- **Live Sold-Out & Markdowns** — Flip a switch and an item vanishes
  from every phone at every table, instantly.
- **Branded QR Generator** — Generate a forest-and-cream QR for every
  table right from the owner dashboard.

## FAQ

- **Does Servd take a cut of my sales?** No. It's a flat monthly rate,
  never a percentage of what you ring in.
- **What happens if I don't confirm an order?** It simply sits in Needs
  Review. Nothing reaches your kitchen until a staff member taps
  Approve — that pause is the whole point.
- **Do my guests need to download an app?** No. The QR opens the menu
  directly in their phone's browser.
- **Can I run this on my existing tablet or do I need new hardware?**
  Any modern tablet or laptop with a browser works for the owner
  dashboard — no special hardware or POS integration required.
- **What if my internet or Wi-Fi drops mid-shift?** Honest limitation:
  Servd needs an internet connection to sync orders in real time. We
  can talk through backup plans for your specific setup on the demo call.
