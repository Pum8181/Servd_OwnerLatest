## Positioning (read this before editing anything else)

Servd is **not a POS replacement** — it's a menu and ordering layer that
sits alongside whatever the restaurant already uses. Payment happens
exactly how it does today, on the restaurant's existing system; Servd
never touches payment.

Two problems it solves, both real, both worth leading with:

1. **Guests order the moment they're ready, not when a server finally
   is.** No flagging someone down, no server having to circle back
   three times just to ask "ready yet?" — the order is already sitting
   there waiting for whoever's free.
2. **Live menu control cuts waste and lost sales.** Instead of a server
   saying "actually, we're out of that" after the fact, the owner flips
   a switch and the item disappears from every phone, live. And instead
   of throwing out food that didn't sell by close, the owner marks it
   down from the dashboard to move it instead.

## The Demo Process (5 steps, the page's centerpiece section)

01. **Guest scans the code at the table** — One tap of their phone
    camera. No app to download, no account to create.
02. **The live menu opens on their phone** — Straight into their
    browser, with the table already attached.
03. **They order, add their name, and choose how to split** — One bill,
    split evenly, or split by what each person ordered.
04. **It waits for staff, not the other way around** — No server has to
    notice the table. Whoever's free confirms it, and can add something
    the guest asked for out loud or pull an out-of-stock item first.
05. **Confirmed, and it's in the kitchen** — Timestamped, tagged with
    who confirmed it. Payment still happens exactly how it does today,
    on the restaurant's existing system.

## FAQ

- **Does this replace my POS or payment system?** No. Servd is a menu
  and ordering layer that sits alongside what you already have — guests
  still pay you exactly how they do today, on your existing system.
  Servd doesn't touch payment at all.
- **Does Servd take a cut of my sales?** No. Flat monthly rate, never a
  percentage of what you ring in.
- **What happens when a guest sends an order?** It lands in Needs
  Review right away — no server has to notice the table. Whoever's free
  confirms it, edits it if needed, and sends it on.
- **Do my guests need to download an app?** No. The QR opens the menu
  directly in their phone's browser.
- **Can I run this on my existing tablet or do I need new hardware?**
  Any modern tablet or laptop with a browser works — no special
  hardware or POS integration required.
- **What if my internet or Wi-Fi drops mid-shift?** Honest limitation:
  Servd needs an internet connection to sync orders in real time. Worth
  talking through backup plans for your specific setup on the demo call.
